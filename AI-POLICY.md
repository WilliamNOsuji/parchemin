Parchemin works fully without AI. You can write, plan, back up and export a whole book without ever turning it on.

## If you turn it on

The AI guide is optional and off by default. You switch it on in settings, and you can switch it off at any time.
Switching it off removes nothing you wrote.

It runs entirely on a server you control. Nobody else can see what you write, and that includes us.

## What it is for

It is a guide and an organizer for your own story. It can:

- ask you questions about your plot, your characters and your chapters
- help you sort your notes, scenes and ideas
- summarize your own chapters back to you, so you can see the shape of the book
- suggest a structure, or a next step when you are stuck

Everything it offers is a suggestion. You decide what to keep.

## It adapts to you

The guide can adapt to your style, so its suggestions fit your book rather than a generic one. It does this on your own
server, from your own writing. What it learns stays there with you.

## What it will never do

- **Write your story.** It does not write chapters, scenes or passages for your book. What it generates is short and
  stays a suggestion.
- **Put words into your manuscript.** Its suggestions stay in their own panel. Nothing it writes enters your story
  unless you type it yourself.
- **Rewrite your prose to "fix" it.** Your sentences stay yours.
- **Share what it learns from you.** Your writing never trains a model for anyone else.
